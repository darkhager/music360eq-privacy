Protos 360 EQ — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The short version

Protos 360 EQ does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

Your EQ profiles, the frequency-response curves you upload, and your hearing-test results are saved only inside the app on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data. Android's own device backup may include them if you have backup turned on; that is controlled by your Google account settings, not by this app.

Files you open or share

When you import or export an EQ file, Android's file picker gives the app access to only the file you choose. When you tap Share, Android's share sheet sends the profile text to the app you pick; what happens next is up to that app.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Modify audio settings — to apply the equalizer to your phone's audio output.
  • Foreground service and notifications — to keep the equalizer running while you use other apps, with a notification showing it is on.
  • Run at startup — to turn the equalizer back on after a restart if you left it on.

The app reads the name of the connected audio output (for example your Bluetooth earbuds) only on your phone, to switch to the profile you linked to it. This name never leaves your device.

Hearing test

The hearing test is a rough listening guide, not a medical device or diagnosis. Its results stay on your phone.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
